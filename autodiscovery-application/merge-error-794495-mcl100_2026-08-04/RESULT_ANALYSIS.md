## Missingness and confounding

Undefinedness is real and class-linked, but the extreme "all-features-undefined"
pathology does not occur here. From the audit, `all_undefined_count` is 0 and
`all_undefined_merges` is 0 — no segment has every feature undefined, so there is
no all-undefined negative pile to trivially separate. That is the healthiest case
for same-brain ROC-AUC honesty.

However, `794495_excludeempty_05_undefined_by_class.png` shows strong
*per-feature* class-conditional missingness: many geometry features (junction
angles, X-crossing dot products, leaf-cluster silhouette) are undefined for
roughly two-thirds of all segments but only ~10% of merges. Whenever a feature is
more often *defined* on merges, the mere fact that it is finite carries label
information, so the model can gain apparent ROC-AUC from the missingness pattern
rather than from the geometric value itself. A large clean population that scores
near zero (visible in `794495_excludeempty_03_score_by_class.png`) can likewise
inflate same-dataset ROC-AUC because ROC rewards separating that easy mass; this
is why AP (0.2982 OOF) is a soberer summary than ROC-AUC (0.8685 OOF).

Two features were deliberately excluded during selection
(`max_normalized_edge_betweenness_bridge`, `max_bridge_radius_variance_ratio`,
via the excluded hypotheses 6 and 39). Excluded features can persist in the
dataset as all-NaN stable columns without being used by the model; they do not
contribute to scoring. The held-out feature heatmap
(`794493_excludeempty_heldout-from-794495_04_feature_ecdf_corr.png`) shows a
different, more clustered correlation structure than the training brain, another
reminder that missingness and correlation are brain-specific confounds.

## Feature interpretation

Figure 06 (`794495_excludeempty_06_winner_model_explanation.png`) uses an
**EBM global term importance** bar chart (mean absolute additive contribution,
unsigned) plus per-term shape functions. EBM importances are unsigned and are not
comparable to a linear coefficient or a tree importance from another family; a
shape function is the additive contribution of that feature to the model score,
not a causal dose-response. Figure 07
(`794495_excludeempty_07_permutation_importance.png`) is the separate AP-scored
validation permutation check. Below, every feature I single out is explained by
what it physically measures and the hypothesis it came from (per
`feature_inventory.json`).

- **`gmm_bimodality_bic_gain`** (hypothesis 14) — the top EBM term and top
  permutation feature. It fits a 1-component vs 2-component Gaussian mixture to
  the distribution of pairwise centroid distances between the segment's large
  (>=50-node) components and reports the BIC improvement of the 2-component model;
  a positive gain means the segment's pieces cluster into two spatially separate
  blobs. Biologically this is the classic false-merge signature: two real neurites
  from different cells fused into one segment show up as two spatial clusters. The
  shape plot rising to a flat high positive score once the gain exceeds ~0 matches
  that intuition (as association, not proof).

- **`max_euclidean_edge_jump`** (hypothesis 8) — the longest single node-to-node
  edge in the skeleton. A merge that stitches two neurites together across a gap
  can leave one abnormally long straight edge; its shape is high at very short
  jumps, dips negative around 6–7um, then slowly recovers, so it is not a simple
  monotone flag.

- **`box_counting_fractal_dimension`** (hypothesis 9) — the 3D box-counting
  fractal dimension of the node cloud (how the point count fills space across box
  scales), computed on large, spread-out components. It captures branching
  space-filling complexity; merged multi-cell segments can have anomalous
  space-filling. Its non-monotone shape (elevated near 0.63–0.67, dipping near
  0.72) means the EBM uses it in a range-specific way.

- **`diameter_to_leaf_ratio`** (hypothesis 37) — the component pseudo-diameter
  length divided by the segment's total leaf count. It contrasts a long backbone
  against how bushy (many-tipped) the segment is; a long path with few leaves
  reads differently from a bushy one. It is a strong EBM term and a top-5
  permutation feature; its shape is positive for small ratios and turns negative
  for large ones.

- **`p95_internode_radius_cv`** (hypothesis 15) — across degree-2 internode
  paths, the value at percentile 95 (the 95th-percentile) of the coefficient of
  variation of node radius along a path. It flags paths where thickness swings
  wildly, which can happen when a merge glues a thick process to a thin one. Its
  shape rises with the feature.

- **`component_spatial_density`** and **`neg_min_log_convexhull_cable_density`**
  (both hypothesis 11 / 31) — density features. `component_spatial_density` is
  cable length divided by bounding-box volume of the largest component;
  `neg_min_log_convexhull_cable_density` is the negated minimum log of cable length
  over convex-hull volume across components, so *low* physical density becomes a
  *high* score. The hypothesis is that a merge inflates the bounding box/hull
  faster than it adds cable, lowering density; both are mid-ranked EBM terms.

- **`neg_min_radius_assortativity`** (hypothesis 30) — the negated minimum radius
  assortativity, i.e. minus the Pearson correlation of node radii across edges;
  low assortativity (poorly correlated neighboring radii) becomes a high score,
  the hypothesis being that merged cables break the smooth radius taper. It is a
  mid EBM term but shows a slightly negative permutation mean, so treat it as
  unstable/redundant rather than a dependable signal.

- **`max_radius_step_along_pseudo_diameter`** (hypothesis 19) — the largest
  absolute radius change between consecutive nodes along the component's
  pseudo-diameter; an abrupt thickness step along the main backbone can mark where
  two processes were joined.

- **`spatial_bimodality_silhouette`** (hypothesis 44) and
  **`max_leaf_cluster_silhouette`** (hypothesis 18) — the two strongest
  permutation features after `gmm_bimodality_bic_gain`. `spatial_bimodality_silhouette`
  is the k=2 KMeans silhouette over all node coordinates of very large (>1000-node)
  components — a direct "does this segment split into two spatial clusters" score.
  `max_leaf_cluster_silhouette` is the k=2 KMeans silhouette of the *leaf-node*
  coordinates within a component, measured only where clustering was genuinely
  possible (hypothesis 18's fix removed the synthetic -1.0 default from
  un-clusterable segments). Both encode the same two-blob merge intuition as
  `gmm_bimodality_bic_gain` and are partly correlated with it, so they split
  credit.

- **`max_windowed_path_tortuosity`** (hypothesis 1) — the maximum sliding-window
  tortuosity (path length over straight-line endpoint distance) over degree-2
  paths; wiggly, indirect paths can arise at a merge join. It is a top-5
  permutation feature.

- **`max_tapering_reversal_rate`** (hypothesis 32) — the fraction of edges where
  the radius *increases* by more than 0.5um while walking outward from the
  thickest node; healthy neurites taper (thin outward), so reversals suggest a
  merge grafted a thick branch onto a thin one. It is a mid-tier permutation
  feature.

For every feature named above, a linear-style sign would be association not
causation, and because several bimodality/density features measure overlapping
geometry they share credit — so no single one should be read as *the* mechanism.
All figure-06 shapes are the final EBM fit on all training rows and are not
held-out evidence.

## Limitations and next steps

- **Held-out status.** A genuine cross-brain held-out test *was* run (`heldout`
  is present, not null): brain `794493`, `mcl100`, 2842 segments, 30 test merges,
  `test_all_undefined_share` 0.0, `mcl_mismatch` false, giving
  `held_out_roc_auc` 0.9166 and `held_out_average_precision` 0.1911. This is real
  generalization evidence and it is the number to trust over the same-brain nested
  CV. It is **not** grounds to claim deployment readiness: the same-brain nested
  CV (OOF AP 0.2982) is optimistic, and a single held-out brain is one point of
  transfer evidence, not a distribution over brains.

- **Cross-brain transfer.** ROC transfers strongly (0.9166) but AP drops to
  0.1911 partly because held-out prevalence is lower (~0.011 vs 0.0304); and
  `794493_excludeempty_heldout-from-794495_04_feature_ecdf_corr.png` shows a
  visibly different correlation structure, so distribution shift is present and
  further brains should be tested before trusting the ranking universally.

- **Convergence warnings.** The detector log recorded 21 convergence warnings
  (`convergence_warning_count` = 21), consistent with the logistic families under
  quasi-separation at 0.0304 prevalence; these did not block the run
  (`completion_line` `# OK after 8930.7s`, `run_complete` true) but they weaken
  confidence in the exact logistic coefficients — another reason to prefer the EBM
  and permutation views over reading a single linear sign.

- **`--exclude-empty` sensitivity.** Scope is `excludeempty`, so segments with no
  measurable geometry were dropped before scoring. Since `all_undefined_count` is
  0 in this scope, the reported metrics do not benefit from an all-undefined
  negative pile — but the metrics are conditional on that exclusion and would
  likely differ (probably lower ROC-AUC) under a keep-empty scope. `skipped_candidates`
  is empty, so no candidate rows were dropped for other reasons.

- **Provenance / reproducibility.** `training_provenance` records
  `model_config_sha256`, `model_policy_sha256`, and `feature_inventory_sha256`,
  but `train_pkl_sha256_recorded` is false and `detector_sha256_recorded_by_training`
  is false. So the saved `merge_detector_794495.joblib` pipeline can be **replayed**
  on new data, but a **bitwise reproduction of training** from the recorded
  provenance cannot be asserted because neither the input pkl nor the detector
  hash was captured.

- **Next steps.** (1) Evaluate on additional held-out brains to characterize
  transfer variance and calibrate expected precision@k. (2) Because the top
  signals (`gmm_bimodality_bic_gain`, `spatial_bimodality_silhouette`,
  `max_leaf_cluster_silhouette`) all encode two-cluster geometry and share credit,
  consider consolidating them to reduce redundancy and stabilize permutation
  importance. (3) Record the input-pkl and detector SHA-256 in future training
  runs to enable bitwise reproduction. (4) If localization (which voxels are
  wrongly joined) is required, note that this detector only *ranks whole segments*
  as merge-suspect and does not localize the merge site.

# 第二部分——中文报告

## 结论摘要

这是针对大脑 `794495`、`mcl100`、范围 `excludeempty` 的 `merge_detection`
检测器，按 `segment` 训练与评估（行单位——每一行是一个重建的分段，而不是一对
被合并的分段，也不是一个候选连接）。其预期用途是**对一次重建的 3227 个分段进行
排序，以便人工校对者优先分诊哪些分段最可能包含错误合并**，而不是给出校准过的
逐分段合并概率，也不是定位出错的体素。正类 `merges` 很稀有：3227 个分段中有
98 个（患病率 0.0304）。最终获胜者是 `explainable_boosting`（一个 EBM），参数为
`{"interactions": 0, "learning_rate": 0.01, "max_bins": 256, "max_rounds": 2000, "min_samples_leaf": 10}`。

最强的结果是该模型可跨大脑迁移：把已拟合的流水线应用到一个真正留出的大脑
`794493`（`mcl100`，2842 个分段，30 个测试合并，患病率约 0.011），得到
`held_out_roc_auc` 0.9166、`held_out_average_precision` 0.1911。在训练大脑上的
嵌套交叉验证中，EBM 家族给出 `oof_roc_auc` 0.8685、`oof_average_precision`
0.2982。排序是有效的：复核 3227 个分段中排名前 500 的分段可找回 98 个合并中的
67 个（召回 0.6837）。

最大的限制是精确度低且任务不平衡：在每一个视角中平均精确度都远低于 ROC-AUC，
队列顶端的精确度最高也只有约 0.46，而高 ROC-AUC 在很大程度上由大量得分接近零的
干净分段与合并之间的易分离所驱动。由于嵌套 CV 和排序数字都来自检测器所拟合的
同一大脑，它们对部署行为而言是乐观估计；跨大脑留出的数字才是诚实的泛化证据，
它们显示出真实的精确度代价（留出 AP 0.1911）。训练溯源记录了配置、策略和特征
清单的哈希，但**没有**记录输入 pkl 的 SHA-256 或检测器的 SHA-256，因此可以重放
所保存的流水线，但无法断言训练的逐位复现。

## 模型比较与选择

选择首先按平均精确度（AP）、其次按 ROC-AUC 对家族排序，因为在 0.0304 的患病率
下 AP 才是关键指标——它概括了决定队列产生多少无效复核的精确度/召回权衡，而
ROC-AUC 仅凭干净-合并之间的易分离就可能显得很强。

五个候选家族的嵌套折外（OOF）比较如下：

| 模型家族 | OOF 行数 | OOF AP | OOF ROC-AUC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `explainable_boosting` | 3227 | 0.2982 | 0.8685 |
| `logistic_elasticnet` | 3227 | 0.2926 | 0.8555 |
| `xgboost` | 3227 | 0.2823 | 0.8576 |
| `hist_gradient_boosting` | 3227 | 0.2722 | 0.8480 |
| `logistic_l2` | 3227 | 0.2512 | 0.8350 |

`explainable_boosting` 同时拥有最高的 AP（0.2982）与最高的 ROC-AUC（0.8685），
因此此处的获胜者也是 AP 最高的家族；两个领先家族（`explainable_boosting`
0.2982 与 `logistic_elasticnet` 0.2926）的 AP 足够接近，其选择处于噪声之内，
这正是一倍标准误规则所要处理的情形——在该规则下，获胜者可以合法地是一个 AP
落在最佳值一倍标准误以内的*更简单*家族，因此最终获胜者不必是唯一 AP 最高的
家族，尽管这里恰好是。

稳定性只是中等。内层选择器在不同折上选中了不同家族：`selection_frequency` 为
`{"explainable_boosting": 2, "logistic_elasticnet": 1, "logistic_l2": 2}`，即没有
任何家族赢下每一折，EBM 和普通 L2 逻辑回归都被选中两次。选择器自身的留出指标
远弱于各家族的 OOF 指标（`selector_metrics`：AP 0.1608、ROC-AUC 0.7459），这是
外层重拟合之前诚实的内环估计，也提醒我们 0.2982 的 OOF AP 是这两个内部数字中更
乐观的一个。这种逐折的家族更替加上 AP 上的接近打平，意味着 EBM 的优势是真实但
微小的；换一个随机种子或折划分，完全有可能反而让 `logistic_elasticnet` 胜出。

## 人工复核工作量

工作量表把获胜者的折外得分列 `merge_probability_oof` 作为一个**排序**来使用，
而不是校准过的概率。它把全部 3227 个分段按得分排序，并统计 98 个真实合并中有
多少落入最先复核的 k 个之内：

| 复核 k | 找到的合并 | Precision@k | OOF 已评分行内的 Recall@k |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 50 | 23 | 0.4600 | 0.2347 |
| 100 | 35 | 0.3500 | 0.3571 |
| 200 | 44 | 0.2200 | 0.4490 |
| 500 | 67 | 0.1340 | 0.6837 |

从操作角度读：一个校对者复核排名前 50 的分段可找到 23 个合并（其打开的分段中
约 46% 是真实合并，并且已捕获全部合并的 23%）；复核 500 个可捕获 98 个合并中的
67 个（68%），但此时打开的分段中只有约 13% 是合并。这是在 0.0304 患病率下预期的
精确度对覆盖率的权衡，而每一个 k 处的精确度都远高于 0.0304 的基线率，因此该排序
显然是有信息量的——但复核者应预期每找到一个合并就要打开若干干净分段。

OOF 得分覆盖是完整的：`oof_coverage` 为 1.0，全部 3227 行都带有有限的
`merge_probability_oof`，`n_oof_unscored` 为 0。由于每个分段都被评分，这里的
`recall_scope` = `oof_scored_rows` 覆盖了该大脑分段的整个候选全域，因此这些召回
数字不会因某个被故意不评分的子集而被稀释。（与采用分段不相交 CV 构建的拆分
检测器不同，这里没有候选行被排除在评分之外；`merge_creating_joins` 列为 `null`，
因为合并检测是对已有分段评分，而非对提议的连接评分。）

## 缺失性与混杂

未定义是真实且与类别相关的，但极端的“全部特征未定义”病态在此并未出现。根据
审计，`all_undefined_count` 为 0，`all_undefined_merges` 为 0——没有任何分段的所有
特征都未定义，因此不存在一个可被轻易分离的全未定义负样本堆。这对同大脑 ROC-AUC
的诚实性来说是最健康的情形。

然而 `794495_excludeempty_05_undefined_by_class.png` 显示出强烈的*逐特征*、类别
条件的缺失：许多几何特征（结点角度、X 交叉点积、叶簇轮廓）对大约三分之二的全部
分段未定义，但对合并仅约 10%。只要某个特征在合并上更常被*定义*，其仅仅是有限值
这一事实就携带标签信息，因此模型可以从缺失模式而非几何值本身获得表面的 ROC-AUC。
一个得分接近零的庞大干净群体（见 `794495_excludeempty_03_score_by_class.png`）
同样会抬高同数据集 ROC-AUC，因为 ROC 奖励把那部分易分质量分离开；这正是为什么
AP（OOF 0.2982）比 ROC-AUC（OOF 0.8685）是更清醒的概括。

在选择过程中有两个特征被故意排除
（`max_normalized_edge_betweenness_bridge`、`max_bridge_radius_variance_ratio`，
经由被排除的假设 6 和 39）。被排除的特征可能作为全 NaN 的稳定列留在数据集中而
不被模型使用；它们不参与评分。留出特征热图
（`794493_excludeempty_heldout-from-794495_04_feature_ecdf_corr.png`）显示出与
训练大脑不同、更成簇的相关结构，这再次提醒缺失性和相关性是与大脑相关的混杂。

## 特征解释

第 06 号图（`794495_excludeempty_06_winner_model_explanation.png`）使用的是
**EBM 全局项重要性** 条形图（平均绝对可加贡献，无符号）加上逐项形状函数。EBM
重要性是无符号的，且不可与线性系数或来自其他家族的树重要性相比较；形状函数是
该特征对模型得分的可加贡献，而不是因果剂量-响应。第 07 号图
（`794495_excludeempty_07_permutation_importance.png`）是单独的、按 AP 评分的
验证置换检验。下面我挑出的每个特征都会说明它物理上度量什么以及它源自哪个假设
（依据 `feature_inventory.json`）。

- **`gmm_bimodality_bic_gain`**（假设 14）—— 排名第一的 EBM 项，也是第一的置换
  特征。它对该分段中较大（>=50 节点）分量的成对质心距离分布拟合 1 分量对 2 分量
  高斯混合，并报告 2 分量模型的 BIC 改善；正增益意味着该分段的碎片聚成两个空间
  上分离的团块。生物学上这是经典的错误合并特征：来自不同细胞的两条真实神经突
  被融合进一个分段，会表现为两个空间簇。形状图在增益超过约 0 后升至平坦的高正
  得分，与此直觉相符（属于关联，而非证明）。

- **`max_euclidean_edge_jump`**（假设 8）—— 骨架中最长的单条节点到节点的边。
  跨越间隙把两条神经突缝合起来的合并可能留下一条异常长的直边；其形状在极短跳跃
  处高，在约 6–7um 处下探为负，然后缓慢恢复，因此并非简单的单调标志。

- **`box_counting_fractal_dimension`**（假设 9）—— 节点云的 3D 盒计数分形维数
  （点数在不同盒尺度上如何填充空间），在大而铺开的分量上计算。它捕捉分支的
  空间填充复杂度；合并的多细胞分段可能有异常的空间填充。其非单调形状（在
  0.63–0.67 附近抬高，在 0.72 附近下探）意味着 EBM 以范围特定的方式使用它。

- **`diameter_to_leaf_ratio`**（假设 37）—— 分量伪直径长度除以该分段的总叶节点
  数。它把一条长骨干与分段的丛生程度（末端数量）作对比；少叶的长路径读起来与
  丛生者不同。它是一个强 EBM 项，也是前 5 名的置换特征；其形状在小比值为正，
  在大比值转负。

- **`p95_internode_radius_cv`**（假设 15）—— 在度数为 2 的节间路径上、沿路径节点
  半径变异系数的第 95 百分位数。它标记厚度剧烈波动的路径，这可能发生在合并把
  一条粗的突起与细的突起粘在一起时。其形状随特征上升。

- **`component_spatial_density`** 与 **`neg_min_log_convexhull_cable_density`**
  （均属假设 11 / 31）—— 密度特征。`component_spatial_density` 是最大分量的电缆
  长度除以外接盒体积；`neg_min_log_convexhull_cable_density` 是各分量中电缆长度
  对凸包体积之比的对数最小值取负，因此*低*物理密度变成*高*得分。假设是合并使
  外接盒/凸包膨胀得比它增加的电缆更快，从而降低密度；两者都是中等排名的 EBM 项。

- **`neg_min_radius_assortativity`**（假设 30）—— 半径同配性最小值取负，即边上
  节点半径 Pearson 相关的负值；低同配性（相邻半径相关性差）变成高得分，假设是
  合并的电缆破坏了平滑的半径渐变。它是中等 EBM 项，但置换均值略为负，因此应视为
  不稳定/冗余而非可靠信号。

- **`max_radius_step_along_pseudo_diameter`**（假设 19）—— 沿分量伪直径相邻节点间
  最大的绝对半径变化；沿主骨干的突兀厚度台阶可能标记两条突起被连接之处。

- **`spatial_bimodality_silhouette`**（假设 44）与
  **`max_leaf_cluster_silhouette`**（假设 18）—— 继 `gmm_bimodality_bic_gain`
  之后最强的两个置换特征。`spatial_bimodality_silhouette` 是对极大（>1000 节点）
  分量的全部节点坐标做 k=2 的 KMeans 轮廓——一个直接的“该分段是否分裂成两个空间
  簇”的得分。`max_leaf_cluster_silhouette` 是分量内*叶节点*坐标的 k=2 KMeans
  轮廓，仅在聚类确实可行处度量（假设 18 的修复移除了无法聚类分段的合成 -1.0
  默认值）。二者都编码了与 `gmm_bimodality_bic_gain` 相同的两团块合并直觉，并与
  之部分相关，因此彼此分摊功劳。

- **`max_windowed_path_tortuosity`**（假设 1）—— 度数为 2 路径上的最大滑动窗口
  弯曲度（路径长度除以端点直线距离）；扭曲、迂回的路径可能出现在合并连接处。
  它是前 5 名的置换特征。

- **`max_tapering_reversal_rate`**（假设 32）—— 从最粗节点向外行走时半径*增加*
  超过 0.5um 的边所占比例；健康的神经突会渐细（向外变细），因此逆转提示合并把
  一条粗分支嫁接到细分支上。它是中档置换特征。

对上面提到的每个特征，线性式的符号是关联而非因果，而且由于若干双峰/密度特征
度量的是重叠的几何，它们分摊功劳——因此不应把任何单一特征读作*那个*机制。所有
第 06 号图的形状都是在全部训练行上的最终 EBM 拟合，不是留出证据。

## 限制与下一步

- **留出状态。** 确实运行了一次真正的跨大脑留出测试（`heldout` 存在，非 null）：
  大脑 `794493`，`mcl100`，2842 个分段，30 个测试合并，`test_all_undefined_share`
  0.0，`mcl_mismatch` 为 false，给出 `held_out_roc_auc` 0.9166 和
  `held_out_average_precision` 0.1911。这是真实的泛化证据，是应优先于同大脑嵌套
  CV 信任的数字。但它**不能**作为宣称可部署的依据：同大脑嵌套 CV（OOF AP
  0.2982）是乐观的，而单个留出大脑只是迁移证据的一个点，而不是跨大脑的分布。

- **跨大脑迁移。** ROC 迁移很强（0.9166），但 AP 降到 0.1911，部分原因是留出
  患病率更低（约 0.011 对 0.0304）；而
  `794493_excludeempty_heldout-from-794495_04_feature_ecdf_corr.png` 显示出明显
  不同的相关结构，因此存在分布漂移，在普遍信任该排序之前应测试更多大脑。

- **收敛警告。** 检测器日志记录了 21 条收敛警告（`convergence_warning_count`
  = 21），这与逻辑回归家族在 0.0304 患病率下的准分离一致；它们没有阻断运行
  （`completion_line` `# OK after 8930.7s`，`run_complete` 为 true），但削弱了对
  确切逻辑回归系数的信心——这也是应优先采用 EBM 和置换视角而非解读单一线性符号的
  又一原因。

- **`--exclude-empty` 敏感性。** 范围为 `excludeempty`，因此没有可测量几何的分段
  在评分前被剔除。由于此范围下 `all_undefined_count` 为 0，所报告的指标并未从
  全未定义负样本堆中获益——但这些指标是以该剔除为条件的，在保留空样本的范围下
  很可能不同（ROC-AUC 大概率更低）。`skipped_candidates` 为空，因此没有候选行
  因其他原因被剔除。

- **溯源/可复现性。** `training_provenance` 记录了 `model_config_sha256`、
  `model_policy_sha256` 和 `feature_inventory_sha256`，但 `train_pkl_sha256_recorded`
  为 false，`detector_sha256_recorded_by_training` 为 false。因此所保存的
  `merge_detector_794495.joblib` 流水线可在新数据上**重放**，但由于既未捕获
  输入 pkl 也未捕获检测器哈希，无法从记录的溯源断言训练的**逐位复现**。

- **下一步。**（1）在更多留出大脑上评估，以刻画迁移方差并校准预期的
  precision@k。（2）由于顶部信号（`gmm_bimodality_bic_gain`、
  `spatial_bimodality_silhouette`、`max_leaf_cluster_silhouette`）都编码两簇几何
  并分摊功劳，可考虑将其整合以减少冗余并稳定置换重要性。（3）在未来的训练运行中
  记录输入 pkl 和检测器的 SHA-256，以支持逐位复现。（4）若需要定位（哪些体素被
  错误连接），请注意本检测器只是把整个分段*排序*为合并可疑，并不定位合并位点。
